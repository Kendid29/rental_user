## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an executive, dependable, and precision-driven digital atmosphere tailored for verified nationwide vehicle mobility. It targets modern Indonesian business professionals, holidaying families, and discerning travelers requiring frictionless, verified vehicle reservations. 

The aesthetic synthesizes high-trust enterprise fintech precision with modern consumer travel tech accessibility. Core visual tenets combine crisp structural order, deliberate geometric typography, and calm tonal canvases with purposeful functional cues. Visual noise is aggressively minimized: trust and authority are signaled through deep executive navy structural hierarchies, pristine white cards, micro-interactions with immediate clarity, and clear semantic validation states.

## Colors

The palette leverages a commanding navy foundation paired with an active digital blue accent to orchestrate clear focal points and seamless transaction paths.

- **Primary (`#102447`):** Deep executive navy. Serves as the primary structural anchor for dominant typography, navigational headers, active step indicators, and high-impact structural UI elements.
- **Secondary (`#0866F5`):** High-clarity primary action blue. Dedicated to key calls-to-action, active radio/checkbox states, focus highlights, active progress nodes, and actionable links.
- **Tertiary (`#00C48C`):** Verified emerald green. Applied strictly to verification badges, vehicle insurance guarantees, instant-confirmation badges, and successful transaction states.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Rating & Alert Amber (`#FFB800`):** Review metrics, star scores, and critical policy notices.
  - **Urgency Coral (`#FF4D4D`):** High-priority alerts, limited-stock vehicle counters, validation errors, and cancellation alerts.
- **Neutral & Structural Foundations:**
  - **Canvas Slate (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, expansive backdrop preventing eye fatigue across data-dense views.
  - **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity pure white for modular cards, sheets, and active panels.
  - **Border Divider (`#E2E8F0`):** Muted slate lineation defining containment without harsh visual breaks.
  - **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Secondary metadata, sub-labels, and input placeholder states.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority of **Outfit** for primary headings with the functional legibility of **Hanken Grotesk** for checkout steps, data specifications, vehicle metadata, and legal policies.

- **Headlines (Outfit):** Geometric cuts and tight tracking convey executive poise. Keep letter-spacing negative on displays above 24px (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to tighten reading paths across broad interfaces.
- **Body & Labels (Hanken Grotesk):** Engineered for dense tabular reading, monetary digits (Indonesian Rupiah formats), and forms. Ensure tabular figures (`tnum`) are enabled for price breakdown ledgers, rental date ranges, and countdown timers.
- **Localization Polish:** Maintain explicit vertical line-height margins to comfortably contain Indonesian compound phrasing (e.g., *Syarat & Ketentuan Pengembalian*, *Detail Pemesanan Kendaraan*) without vertical truncation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptable 12-column fluid grid system with structural break points:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, dynamic margins bounded by a max container width of 1240px. The reservation flow implements an asymmetric layout: an 8-column main interaction area (vehicle forms, driver documents, payment method selectors) paired with a 4-column sticky checkout order summary.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px canvas margins. The sticky summary converts into a collapsible floating summary panel pinned to the viewport bottom.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margins. Linear stacked order flow with full-width primary interactions.

Rhythm is strictly anchored to a 4px/8px incremental spatial scale. Interior component padding scales symmetrically (`space-sm` to `space-lg`), while distinct transactional modules (e.g., Driver Details vs. Payment Channels) are separated by `space-xl` (32px) margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines surface tinting with soft, cool-navy ambient drop shadows, discarding flat monotony while avoiding heavy skeuomorphism.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral `#F8FAFC` base canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting Surface):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a crisp structural hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(16, 36, 71, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(16, 36, 71, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Sticky Elements):** Used for the sticky order summary widget, focused input groups, and hovered vehicle cards: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(16, 36, 71, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 36, 71, 0.04)`. Border shifts to `rgba(8, 102, 245, 0.2)` upon active selection.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Used for document zoom viewports (KTP/SIM upload previews) and payment channel confirmation bottom-sheets: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(16, 36, 71, 0.16)`. Background backdrop blur is set to `blur(6px)` with `rgba(16, 36, 71, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Roundedness 2**:
- **Standard Cards & Containers:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem radius) to deliver clean framing for car listings, vehicle spec matrices, and multi-option checkout groups.
- **Form Controls & Action Buttons:** Anchored at 8px to 10px corner radiuses, maintaining crisp legibility and functional alignment.
- **Pill Elements:** Full rounded radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px) are strictly reserved for status chips, step indicator nodes, badge tags (*Terverifikasi*, *Lepas Kunci*, *Dengan Sopir*), and compact category chips.

## Components

### Multi-Step Checkout Progress Bar
- **Stages:** `Detail Kendaraan` > `Checkout & Pembayaran` > `Selesai`.
- **Visuals:** Centered horizontal sequence. Active steps display a solid `#0866F5` circular node with an inner white glyph, connected by an active 2px `#0866F5` line. Inactive upcoming steps feature an `#E2E8F0` border ring with `#64748B` numerical indicators. Completed steps convert to an emerald green `#00C48C` checkmark.

### Sticky Order Summary Widget
- Fixed right-hand card desktop widget (`rounded-2xl`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, Elevation Level 2).
- Includes vehicle thumbnail preview, transmission badge, date/time pickup & return badges, transparent itemized ledger (Sewa Kendaraan, Biaya Proteksi/Asuransi, Pajak), and bold `#102447` total payable sum with active CTA.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-clarity `#0866F5` background, pure white text, bold font weight, 10px border radius, subtle hover elevation shift, and active state scaled down (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `#102447` text, transitioning to `#F1F5F9` background on hover.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent surface with `#0866F5` text, zero horizontal padding for inline link triggers.

### Payment Channel Selectors
- Dedicated accordions and radio card tiles for Indonesian payment gateways:
  - **Virtual Account:** BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI, Permata.
  - **Instant Pay:** QRIS (prominent dynamic preview container).
  - **Kartu Kredit:** Visa, Mastercard, JCB with security badges.
  - **E-Wallet:** GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay.
  - **PayLater & Gerai:** Indomaret, Alfamart, Kredivo, Akulaku.
- Resting state features `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Selected state triggers a `2px solid #0866F5` stroke, soft `#0866F508` background tint, and active radio checkmark.

### Form Inputs
- Height constrained to 48px with 12px internal horizontal padding.
- Crisp `#E2E8F0` resting border, shifting to `#0866F5` on `:focus-visible` with a 3px outer glow `rgba(8, 102, 245, 0.15)`.
- Explicit floating labels with Indonesian validation cues (*cth. Sesuai KTP*). Error states utilize `#FF4D4D` with immediate inline corrective messaging.

### Pill Badges & Chips
- **Verified/Safe:** `#00C48C` tint background (`#E6F9F3`) with dark emerald `#00875A` text and shield icon.
- **Rating:** `#FFF9E6` background with `#B27B00` text and an amber `#FFB800` star icon.
- **Urgency/Availability:** `#FEE2E2` background with `#DC2626` text for remaining fleet alerts (*Sisa 1 Mobil!*).
